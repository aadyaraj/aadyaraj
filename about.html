About Me

Hello! I'm Aadya Raj, a passionate Data Analyst based in Bangalore, India, with over 2 years of experience in data analysis and reporting, alongside 1.6 years of prior experience in client-facing operations.

Currently, as a Process Executive/Analyst at Cognizant (Client: Google), I specialize in analyzing complex application and operational data, identifying trends, and generating actionable business insights. I have hands-on expertise with large-scale datasets, having processed and standardized over 100,000 records from multiple regions and data sources.

My toolkit heavily features SQL, Advanced Excel, Power BI, and Python (Pandas). I pride myself on not just querying data, but building intuitive dashboards and reporting templates that drive efficiency. For example, my recent Excel reporting templates successfully reduced reporting turnaround time by 40% across four departments.

My background in Customer Operations has instilled a deep appreciation for quality and accuracy. I have consistently maintained 95%+ accuracy and SLA adherence while handling high-volume reviews and conducting root-cause analysis on recurring performance issues.

Beyond the numbers, my goal is always clear: to translate raw data into stories that empower stakeholders to make confident, data-driven decisions.
